Featured project

Winds of Valor

Teesside University · BA Games Design final project · First Class Honours · 2025

A third-person melee combat game inspired by Elden Ring and Ghost of Tsushima. I designed and built the systems below in Unreal Engine.

Combat component

  • Modular attack/defence Actor Component: light, heavy and charged attacks, block, parry, dodge and lock-on.
  • Works on any UE third-person character through retargeting.
  • Animation-driven hit registration with per-skeleton-part critical hits.

Stats layer

  • Health, Stamina, Stagger and Action stats.
  • Data tables for tuning.

Enemy AI

  • Perception, plus state and action slots with action context.
  • A manager decides who presses the attack and who flanks.
  • Combat-sports-style movement: circling at different distances, strafing, and angled or direct approaches, with a cadence-based choice between circle, angle, strafe and charge.
  • Weighted-chance triggers for attack, defend and dodge.
  • EQS keeps enemies from bunching up. A pre-attack check stops them from hitting teammates.

Feel & feedback

  • Diegetic health bar, damage numbers and a combo style meter.
  • Hit stop, VFX and material effects.
  • Purchased SFX wired to gameplay triggers.

UI & flow

  • Main menu and pause/escape menu.
  • Narrator text-bubble system.
